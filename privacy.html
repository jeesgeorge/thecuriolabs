Curious Lab Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Curious Lab Publisher is a private tool used only by The Curious Lab (Instagram @thecuriolabs, and The Curious Labs on Pinterest) to publish its own short videos and to read the insights, comments and messages on its own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

Through the Instagram API, and only for @thecuriolabs:

  • basic profile information and our own posts (to publish and list them);
  • insights on our own posts (views, reach, likes, shares, saves);
  • comments on our own posts and messages sent to our account (so we can read and answer them).

Through the Pinterest API, and only for our own Pinterest business account:

  • basic account information and our own boards (to choose where a Pin goes);
  • creating Pins on our own boards from our own videos;
  • basic analytics on our own Pins.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish our content and to understand and reply to our audience. It is stored on one private computer, is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Comment and message text is kept only as long as needed to reply. Insight summaries are kept for our own analytics. Access tokens are stored privately on that computer and can be revoked at any time from the Instagram or Pinterest account settings. To ask for any data about you to be deleted (for example a comment you left on our posts), message @thecuriolabs on Instagram or email jeesgeorge@gmail.com with the word "DELETE", and it will be removed within 30 days.
